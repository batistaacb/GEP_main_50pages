                \textbf{\textcolor{PrimaryGold}{The Real Problem:}} \\

                Supermarkets still depend on static, manually updated price tags. \\
                This creates operational work and errors while making frequent price changes \\
                 difficult. Solving this problem can unlock smarter pricing.
            };

            % Topic 3: Our Solution

            \node[topicbox, below=0.1cm of problem, fill=GoldCard, draw=PrimaryGold, align=left] (solution) {

                \textbf{\textcolor{PrimaryGreen}{Our Solution:}} \\

                We replaced the multi-step barcode journey with one QR code that opens the \\
                retailer's WhatsApp conversation with the product code already attached. Scan \\
                \& Go and AI commerce became the tools. Solving the price-tag problem and \\
                enabling dynamic pricing became our focus.
            };
